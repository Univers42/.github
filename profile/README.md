## About Univers42

Univers42 is an independent engineering community bringing together developers, students and engineers who want to build beyond the boundaries of traditional academic projects.

We provide an environment where people can collaborate on real software products, develop reusable engineering solutions, exchange ideas, challenge architectural decisions and grow their professional skills.

We believe that software engineering is much more than writing code. It involves identifying problems, understanding existing systems, making technical decisions, collaborating with others and building solutions that can evolve over time.

Companies increasingly value people who can demonstrate that they know how to work within engineering environments, contribute to existing projects, identify opportunities for improvement and turn ideas into practical solutions.

**That's the kind of experience we want to encourage at Univers42.**

What makes this ecosystem worth joining isn't a particular technology, programming language or academic background. It's the willingness of contributors to build, learn, share knowledge and collaborate.

## Our Philosophy

We believe good engineering comes from open-mindedness, experimentation and continuous improvement.

There is no development methodology that is inherently superior to another. Whether you prefer traditional programming, AI-assisted development, agentic workflows, manual coding or a combination of these approaches, what matters is the quality of the engineering outcome.

We welcome different perspectives, tools and approaches. What we expect is the ability to understand your decisions and take responsibility for the software you build.

### Engineering Over Implementation

Writing code is only one part of software engineering.

Architecture, maintainability, reproducibility, scalability, testing, documentation and the ability to understand technical trade-offs are equally important.

We encourage contributors to:

- Challenge existing approaches and propose alternatives.
- Experiment with new technologies and development methodologies.
- Improve existing architectures and implementations.
- Share technical knowledge and explain their decisions.
- Contribute to existing projects through issues, discussions and pull requests.
- Build new solutions when existing ones don't address the problem.

We don't expect every project to start with a perfect architecture. We expect contributors to recognize opportunities for improvement and be willing to work on them.

**You can improve something that already exists, fork a project, propose a new direction or start building something entirely new.**

There is always room for a better solution.

## What You Can Build at Univers42

**There are no predefined technological or thematic restrictions.**

We don't want to limit creativity to a particular programming language, framework, discipline or technology.

Whether you're interested in systems programming, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, games, infrastructure or business applications, you're welcome to explore your ideas here.

What matters is that your project brings reusable value, solves a problem, explores an interesting engineering challenge or provides something other developers can learn from.

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Software Components** | Libraries, frameworks, functions, plugins, SDKs and reusable modules |
| **Complete Products** | SaaS platforms, B2B applications, business tools and software products |
| **Infrastructure & Systems** | Operating systems, networking tools, DevOps, automation and infrastructure solutions |
| **AI & Agentic Engineering** | AI agents, harnesses, orchestration systems and AI-powered applications |
| **Experimental Projects** | Games, cybersecurity tools, research implementations and technical prototypes |

Projects can range from a small reusable library to a complete software platform. Their scope isn't what determines their value.

**We care about the engineering challenge, the problem being addressed and the value the project can bring to others.**

## What We're Looking For

Univers42 is open to different technical backgrounds and experience levels.

We're interested in people who want to develop their engineering skills, contribute to a collaborative environment and participate in building something meaningful.

### Technical Interests

Some of the areas we're particularly interested in include:

- Software engineering and architecture.
- AI engineering and agentic development.
- Cybersecurity and information security.
- Quality assurance, testing and reliability.
- Systems programming and infrastructure.
- Full-stack and backend development.
- Automation and developer tooling.
- Cloud computing and DevOps.

These are examples, not requirements. You don't need to specialize in any particular field to participate.

### Personal Qualities

Beyond technical skills, we value people who:

- Enjoy exchanging ideas and discussing technical challenges.
- Question existing solutions and think independently.
- Accept constructive criticism and alternative perspectives.
- Are willing to share knowledge and help others progress.
- Want to turn ideas into actual products and solutions.
- Understand that collaboration is an engineering skill.
- Take initiative instead of waiting for everything to be assigned.

You don't need to be an expert to participate. Junior developers, intermediate developers, students and experienced engineers can all bring something valuable to the community.

**We value curiosity, initiative and willingness to improve over titles or years of experience.**

## What Univers42 Is Not

We want to build an environment focused on meaningful engineering contributions rather than simply accumulating repositories.

Not every project needs to be production-ready, but it should have a purpose beyond existing as another item in someone's portfolio.

| Welcome | Not the Objective |
|---|---|
| Reusable libraries and components | One-off implementations with no reusable value |
| Experimental prototypes and proof of concepts | Projects created exclusively to inflate portfolios |
| Personal software products | Hardcoded solutions that only work in one specific situation |
| AI-assisted applications | Applications nobody understands, maintains or can reproduce |
| Research and technical exploration | Abandoned experiments without documentation or meaningful outcomes |

We don't reject experimentation, imperfect implementations or ambitious ideas.

We reject the mindset that publishing code is the same as building something valuable.

### The Kind of Environment We Want to Avoid

Technical ability alone isn't enough to make a community work.

We don't want an environment dominated by:

- People who believe they already have the answer to everything.
- Toxic behaviour, unnecessary ego or disrespect towards contributors.
- People who discourage experimentation or dismiss others' approaches without constructive arguments.
- Individuals unwilling to communicate, collaborate or accept feedback.
- Perfectionism that prevents projects from progressing.

We encourage high engineering standards, but we don't confuse high standards with gatekeeping.

**The objective is to help each other build better software, not to prove who knows the most.**

## What We Expect From Members

Joining Univers42 means becoming part of an environment where people are expected to contribute with intention.

We don't require everyone to contribute every day or dedicate a fixed number of hours to the organization.

However, we encourage members to:

- Participate in discussions and share ideas.
- Document projects, implementations and technical decisions.
- Be open to feedback and alternative approaches.
- Respect other people's contributions and perspectives.
- Help improve existing projects whenever possible.
- Take ownership of the work they decide to undertake.
- Communicate progress, blockers and important changes.

You don't have to contribute every day, but we expect you to contribute with intention.

A community becomes valuable when its members actively exchange knowledge, help each other overcome challenges and turn discussions into actual progress.

## What You Get in Return

We're offering more than a place to publish code.

**We're offering an opportunity to build a professional engineering track record.**

By participating in Univers42, you can benefit from:

- **Project Visibility:** Opportunities to have your projects and contributions featured through Univers42's communication channels, including LinkedIn.
- **Professional Exposure:** Increased visibility among developers, engineers, companies and potential collaborators.
- **Real Engineering Experience:** Opportunities to participate in collaborative software products and work with different engineering disciplines.
- **Technical Growth:** Exposure to architectural discussions, code reviews, alternative implementations and real engineering challenges.
- **Networking:** Connections with people who share an interest in building software and developing new ideas.
- **Product Development:** Opportunities to collaborate on ambitious projects, explore startup ideas and potentially turn them into real products.

### Growing Together

As Univers42 continues to grow its visibility, community and professional network, we want our contributors to benefit from that growth.

We intend to actively promote outstanding projects, highlight meaningful contributions and share the work being developed within the organization.

This means giving contributors additional exposure, recognition and opportunities to connect with companies and professionals.

Your work shouldn't have to disappear into a repository that nobody sees.

**When the community grows, the people building it should grow with it.**

## How We Collaborate

We use different platforms depending on the nature of the collaboration.

| Platform | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **GitHub** | Source code, documentation, issues, pull requests and technical discussions |
| **Discord** | Community conversations, informal exchanges and voice meetings |
| **Slack** | Project coordination and team communication where appropriate |

### GitHub Discussions: Our Knowledge Hub

We strongly encourage contributors to use GitHub Discussions whenever possible.

Why?

Because knowledge should remain connected to the projects and decisions it concerns.

Architectural decisions made exclusively through private Discord conversations can become difficult for future contributors to understand or recover.

GitHub Discussions provides a centralized place to:

- Propose new projects and functionalities.
- Discuss architectural decisions.
- Share technical ideas and research.
- Ask questions and exchange knowledge.
- Coordinate contributions and improvements.

We want discussions to become part of the project's history rather than disappear into disconnected conversations.

## Getting Involved

Getting started doesn't need to be complicated.

1. **Explore the organization:** Browse our repositories and discover what is already being developed.
2. **Find something interesting:** Identify a project, technology or engineering challenge that interests you.
3. **Introduce yourself:** Share your background, interests and what you'd like to contribute.
4. **Join the discussions:** Participate in existing conversations, propose improvements and exchange ideas.
5. **Bring your own ideas:** If something doesn't exist yet, propose a new project through GitHub Discussions.

You don't need to arrive with a fully developed product or a perfect roadmap.

You just need an idea, the willingness to work on it and an open mind towards collaboration.
