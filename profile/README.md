## 🌍 What is Univers42?

Univers42 is an independent community where developers collaborate to build **real software, reusable tools and ambitious products.**

We bring together people who want to go beyond completing academic exercises and experience what software engineering looks like in real collaborative environments.

💡 Our philosophy is simple:

- Build things that solve problems.
- Improve what already exists.
- Share knowledge and challenge ideas.
- Turn interesting discussions into actual projects.
- Help each other grow as engineers.

> Companies value people who can collaborate, identify problems, propose solutions and contribute to existing engineering environments.
>
> **Don't just learn how to complete projects. Learn how to build software that others can use, understand and improve.**

## 🧠 Our Philosophy

We are technology-agnostic and open-minded.

Whether you prefer traditional programming, AI-assisted development, agentic workflows or manual coding, **we don't judge the methodology; we care about the engineering outcome.**

What matters to us:

- 🏗️ Good architecture and maintainability.
- 🔄 Reproducibility and scalability.
- 🧪 Testing, reliability and documentation.
- 💬 Open discussions and constructive criticism.
- 🚀 Continuous improvement and experimentation.

You don't need to start with a perfect implementation. You need to be willing to make it better.

**Found something that could be improved? Propose it. Missing something? Let's build it.**

## 🛠️ What Can You Build?

There are no predefined technological or thematic restrictions.

If it brings value, solves a problem or explores an interesting engineering challenge, it belongs here.

| Field | Examples |
|---|---|
| 🧩 Software Components | Libraries, frameworks, plugins, SDKs |
| 🚀 Products | SaaS, B2B platforms, business applications |
| ⚙️ Systems & Infrastructure | Networking, operating systems, DevOps, automation |
| 🤖 AI & Agentic Engineering | Agents, harnesses, orchestration, AI applications |
| 🎮 Experimental Projects | Games, cybersecurity, research, prototypes |

From a small reusable function to an entire software platform: **we welcome ambitious ideas and practical solutions.**

## 👥 Who Are We Looking For?

You don't need to be an expert. Junior developers, intermediate engineers, students and experienced professionals are welcome.

### 💻 Technical Interests

- Software engineering & architecture
- AI engineering & agentic development
- Cybersecurity
- QA & testing
- Systems programming & infrastructure
- Full-stack & backend development
- DevOps & automation

### ❤️ Personal Qualities

We appreciate people who:

- Enjoy exchanging ideas and sharing knowledge.
- Question existing solutions.
- Accept constructive criticism.
- Take initiative and turn ideas into action.
- Respect other contributors and their work.
- Understand that collaboration is an engineering skill.

### 🚫 What We Don't Want

- Toxic behaviour and unnecessary ego.
- People unwilling to communicate or collaborate.
- Dogmatic attitudes towards technologies and methodologies.
- People who criticize everything but never contribute.
- Projects created exclusively to inflate portfolios.

**High standards, yes. Gatekeeping, no.**

## 🤝 What We Expect From You

Being part of Univers42 means contributing with intention.

We don't expect daily contributions or a fixed number of hours. We do expect people to:

- 💬 Participate in discussions.
- 📝 Document projects and technical decisions.
- 🔍 Be open to feedback and improvements.
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Respect and support other contributors.
- 🎯 Take ownership of the work they undertake.

> You don't have to contribute every day. Just make your contributions count.

## 🎁 What Do You Get in Return?

We're offering more than another place to publish your code.

We're building an ecosystem where your work can become part of your professional journey.

| Benefit | What it means |
|---|---|
| 📢 Visibility | Opportunities to feature your projects on Univers42's LinkedIn and communication channels. |
| 🌐 Networking | Connect with developers, engineers, companies and potential collaborators. |
| 🏗️ Experience | Work on real software products and collaborative engineering projects. |
| 🧠 Growth | Exchange knowledge, receive feedback and improve your technical skills. |
| 💼 Opportunities | Build a stronger professional track record and increase your exposure to potential employers. |
| 🚀 Entrepreneurship | Explore ideas, collaborate on products and potentially build your own startup. |

As Univers42 grows, we want our contributors to grow with it.

**Your work deserves to be seen. We want to help make that happen.**

## 💬 How We Collaborate

We use different platforms for different purposes.

| Platform | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 🐙 GitHub | Code, documentation, issues, pull requests and technical discussions. |
| 💬 Discord | Community conversations, informal exchanges and voice meetings. |
| ⚡ Slack | Project coordination where appropriate. |

### Why GitHub Discussions?

We strongly encourage using GitHub Discussions as our central knowledge hub.

Architectural decisions and technical discussions shouldn't disappear into private conversations.

Keeping knowledge close to the code makes projects easier to understand, maintain and contribute to.

## 🚀 Getting Involved

Getting started is simple:

1. 🔎 Explore our repositories.
2. 💡 Find a project that interests you.
3. 👋 Introduce yourself and your ideas.
4. 💬 Join existing discussions.
5. 🛠️ Propose a new project or contribute to an existing one.

You don't need a perfect roadmap or an established product.

**Bring your curiosity, your ideas and your willingness to build.**
